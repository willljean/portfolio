About Me

I'm William Leduc Jean, a Computer Science student at the University of Ottawa and an aspiring developer. I have always taken an interest to UI/UX design, the intersection of design and development, and graphic design as a whole. These are all reasons why I am taking the SEG3125 course, and why I am excited to continue applying what I'll learn to this website and my future projects.

How I Work

Research Structure Build Refine

I approach projects by starting with visual research, then shaping a clear structure before moving into code. I like working iteratively: building a usable version first, testing how it feels, and refining the interface until the design and functionality support each other.

This portfolio was built the same way, through small experiments with layout, identity, motion, and interaction.
